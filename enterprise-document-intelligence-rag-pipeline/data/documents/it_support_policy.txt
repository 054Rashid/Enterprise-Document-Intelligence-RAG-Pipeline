IT Support Policy

Employees should create an IT support ticket for laptop, software, account-access, and connectivity issues. Tickets should include a short description, business impact, screenshots when useful, and the employee's contact information.

Critical issues affecting a major business process are prioritized ahead of standard requests. The IT team targets an initial response within four business hours for standard support tickets.

Employees should not share passwords or authentication codes in support tickets. For suspected security incidents, the employee should immediately contact the security response team through the approved incident channel.
