Procurement Policy

Purchases above the department's approval threshold require manager approval before an order is placed. Vendor invoices should reference the purchase order number when a purchase order exists.

Finance reconciles invoices against purchase orders and receiving records before payment. Exceptions such as missing purchase orders, duplicate invoices, or amount mismatches are routed for investigation.

Vendor changes must be validated before payment information is updated. Employees should use approved procurement channels for new vendors and purchasing requests.
