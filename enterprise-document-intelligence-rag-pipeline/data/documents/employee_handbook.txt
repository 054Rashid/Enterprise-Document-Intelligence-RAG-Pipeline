Employee Handbook

Annual leave requests should be submitted through the HR portal at least five business days before planned leave when practical. Managers review requests based on team coverage and operational needs.

New employees complete onboarding tasks during their first two weeks. The HR team provides policy information, payroll guidance, benefits enrollment instructions, and required compliance training.

Employees should keep their personal and payroll information current in the HR system. Questions about benefits can be raised with the People Operations team through the HR service desk.
